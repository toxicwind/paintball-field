# SpectreBand Brand

> **"Every field is a battlefield. Every player is a ghost."**

## Mythos

SpectreBand was born from a simple observation: paintball is supposed to feel like combat, but it plays like tag. You get hit, you raise your hand, you walk to the dead box. No stakes. No intel. No teamwork beyond yelling "he's over there!"

The name comes from the **Spectre** — a ghost that haunts the battlefield after death. In SpectreBand, eliminated players don't sit out. They become invisible observers who relay intel to their living teammates. Death is not an ending. It is a role change.

**GhostNet** extends this mythos: the band itself is a ghost — a silent presence on your wrist that only speaks when something important happens. No screen glow to give away your position. No radar to replace your eyes. Just haptic pulses and LED flashes that mean something if you know the code.

## Core Principles

1. **Technology serves the game, not replaces it.** Bands enhance awareness. They don't replace looking downrange, listening for footsteps, or communicating with your team.
2. **Death is a transition, not a timeout.** Every mode keeps eliminated players engaged. Ghosts spot. Wounded players need extraction. The game doesn't end when you get hit.
3. **The referee is the game master.** One ref with 20 players is impossible. Our systems give refs the tools to monitor, validate, and narrate the game — not replace their judgment.
4. **Field owners profit fairly.** $168 kit. $8 add-on. Payback in 3 sessions. No subscriptions. No lock-in. MIT license.
5. **No phones on the field.** Mud, paint, drops, theft, liability. Dedicated hardware only.

## Tone

- **Direct.** No corporate speak. No "synergy." Say what it does.
- **Tactical.** Paintball players speak in shorthand. So do we.
- **Honest.** If a mode is hard, say it's hard. If the battery dies in 4 hours, say 4 hours.
- **Inclusive.** Works for 8-year-olds at a birthday party and 28-year-olds in a tournament.

## Visual Identity

- **Primary:** Tactical olive drab (#4B5320) + warning orange (#FF6B35)
- **Secondary:** Ghost white (#F8F8FF) + night black (#0A0A0A)
- **LED palette:** Team Alpha = blue (#0066CC), Team Bravo = red (#CC0000), Neutral = white (#FFFFFF), Alert = orange (#FF6600), Emergency = red pulse (#FF0000)
- **Typography:** Monospace for data (scores, timers, player IDs). Sans-serif for everything else.

## Naming Conventions

- **Products:** GhostNet (entry), SpectreBand (premium)
- **Hardware:** GhostBand, GhostNode, GhostHub, SpectreBand, SpectreNode
- **Modes:** Action nouns (Blackhawk Down, Dead Drop, King of the Hill). No numbers.
- **Teams:** Alpha / Bravo (not Red / Blue — colorblind friendly)
- **Status:** Alive, Eliminated, Wounded, Ghost (not Dead — too final)
